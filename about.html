My Story

Hello, I'm Finito911, an artist with a passion for capturing emotion through visual storytelling. My journey in art began at a young age when I discovered the power of colors and shapes to express what words could not.

Over the years, I've developed a unique style that blends traditional techniques with contemporary themes. My work often explores the relationship between nature and human emotion, seeking to find beauty in the ordinary moments of life.

I believe that art has the power to connect people across cultures and experiences. Each piece I create is a reflection of my personal growth and understanding of the world around me.

Education & Exhibitions

  • Featured Artist, Contemporary Art Gallery, NYC (2018)
  • Group Exhibition, International Art Fair, Paris (2020)
